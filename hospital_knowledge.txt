[General Hospital Guidelines & Clinical Knowledge Base]

1. Normal Vital Signs:
- Blood Pressure: Systolic 90-120 mmHg, Diastolic 60-80 mmHg.
- Heart Rate: 60-100 bpm.
- Fasting Blood Sugar: 70-99 mg/dL.
- SpO2: 95% - 100%.

2. Common Chronic Conditions & Standard Management:
- Hypertension: Monitor BP twice daily, reduce sodium intake, recommend ACE inhibitors or ARBs as initial therapy if indicated.
- Type 2 Diabetes: Target HbA1c < 7.0%, recommend Metformin first-line unless contraindicated (e.g., eGFR < 30).
- Hyperlipidemia: Lifestyle modifications, statin therapy based on cardiovascular risk score.

3. Red Flags & Urgent Consultation:
- Severe acute chest pain, shortness of breath at rest, persistent fever > 39°C, neurological deficits.
